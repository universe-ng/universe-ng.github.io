Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

UniVerse has a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and exploitation. We do not tolerate any content that depicts, promotes, or normalizes sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

2. Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report inappropriate content directly within the app using the "Report" button on any post or profile. Reports related to child safety are prioritized for immediate review.

3. Cooperation with Authorities

If we identify any CSAM or exploitation on our platform, we will:

  • Immediately remove the content.
  • Ban the offending account permanently.
  • Report the incident to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant Nigerian law enforcement agencies.
