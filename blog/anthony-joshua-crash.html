Anthony Joshua Involved in Tragic Car Crash in Nigeria

By BTK • December 30, 2025

Sad news: British Nigerian boxing champion Anthony Joshua was involved in a serious car accident on the Lagos Ibadan Expressway on December 29, 2025.

Two of his close friends and team members strength coach Sina Ghami and trainer Kevin Ayodele tragically lost their lives.

Joshua is in stable condition and receiving treatment. The crash happened while he was visiting family in Ogun State during the holidays.

The Lagos Ibadan road is known for heavy traffic and accidents a reminder to drive safe this season.

Our thoughts with AJ, the families, and everyone affected.
