EFCC Chair: "Nigeria Will Rise Again" in 2026

By BTK • January 1, 2026

As 2025 ends, EFCC Chairman Ola Olukoyede delivered a powerful New Year message on December 31: "Nigeria will rise again" but only through collective integrity and zero tolerance for corruption.

He tied the hope to President Tinubu's Renewed Hope agenda, saying Nigerians must reject corruption at all levels from small bribes to grand theft.

Key quotes:

  • "Corruption is the biggest enemy of progress"
  • "2026 must be the year we say 'enough is enough'"
  • "Every citizen has a role report, refuse, resist"

Many students see this as hope especially with economic hardship in 2025. Will 2026 bring real change?
