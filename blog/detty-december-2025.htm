Detty December 2025: Lagos Never Sleeps

By BTK • December 30, 2025

Detty December is in full swing and 2025 is bigger than ever!

Diaspora Nigerians return home, cash flows, parties back-to-back. From beach raves to church carol services to midnight clubbing Lagos is the place to be.

Highlights this year:

  • Afrobeats stars performing everywhere
  • Traffic? Legendary
  • Wads of cash and designer outfits
  • Everyone posting "Home for the holidays" stories

Whether you're in Lagos or watching from campus the energy is contagious.
