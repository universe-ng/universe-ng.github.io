Security Concerns Carry into 2026 Christmas Attacks Still in Focus

By BTK • January 1, 2026

As we begin 2026 with hope, two major security incidents from Christmas week remain fresh in our minds and continue to spark national conversation.

Christmas Eve Bombing in Maiduguri

On December 24, 2025, a suspected suicide bomber attacked a mosque in Maiduguri during evening prayers. At least 5 worshippers killed, over 35 injured.

Borno State has seen relative calm in recent years, making this attack particularly shocking. Authorities blame Boko Haram remnants.

US Airstrikes in Sokoto

On Christmas Day, US forces launched airstrikes on alleged ISIS-linked camps in Sokoto State's forests the first direct US military action on Nigerian soil in years.

Coordinated with Nigerian government, targeting foreign jihadists. Some villagers reported panic from falling debris.

What This Means for Students

Many Nigerian university students have family in the north. These events bring real fear especially for those traveling home or with siblings in secondary school.

While overall security has improved in some areas, these incidents show threats persist.

Safety tips for 2026:

  • Avoid crowded places when possible
  • Report suspicious activity
  • Stay connected with family
  • Use apps like UniVerse to share local updates
