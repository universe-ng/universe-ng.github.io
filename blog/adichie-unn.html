Chimamanda Adichie Denies UNN Visiting Professor Appointment

By BTK • December 28, 2025

Drama alert: UNN announced Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie as a visiting professor on December 18, 2025 — but she says it's 100% false.

Her team released a statement: "Ms. Adichie has not received any such appointment, nor has she been in communication with the University." The reports about official letters? "Inaccurate."

UNN hasn't backed down yet, saying the appointment stands and it's up to her to accept/decline.

Students are confused (and memeing hard). Who’s your dream visiting lecturer for your department?
