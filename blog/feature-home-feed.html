How to Use the UniVerse Home Feed Your Campus in One Place 🚀

By BTK • January 1, 2026

Welcome to 2026! 🎉

First day of the new year perfect time to learn how the Home Feed works. This is where everything happens on UniVerse: posts, stories, channels, anonymous, and updates from your university mates.

1. The Stories Row (Top of Feed)

At the very top, you'll see circular profile pictures these are Stories from people in your university.

  • Red/orange ring = new stories to watch
  • Tap any circle to view full screen stories (with video, reactions, and viewers)
  • Your own story bubble is first (with + sign) tap to post your own!

2. The Main Feed

Below stories this is where the real action is:

  • Posts from people in your university (chronological, no algorithm nonsense)
  • Like ❤️, reply 💬, repost 🔄, or report if needed
  • Tap username or avatar to visit profile
  • Long-press post for more options (copy, share link)

3. Channels Tab (Bottom Navigation)

Swipe right or tap the Channels icon to see organized groups:

  • Department channels (e.g., Computer Science 300L)
  • Hostel, events, buy & sell, gist everything separated
  • Join or create your own!

Why the Feed is Better Than WhatsApp Groups

  • No message flooding posts stay visible
  • Stories disappear after 24hrs (perfect for quick updates)
  • Everything stays within your university safe and relevant

The Home Feed is your campus timeline. Open the app → you're instantly connected.
