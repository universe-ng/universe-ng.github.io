FG & ASUU Finally End 16-Year Renegotiation Crisis

By BTK • December 29, 2025

Historic moment: On December 23, 2025, Federal Government and ASUU signed a new agreement ending the renegotiation that started in 2009!

No more endless strikes over the same issues? 🤞

Key points:

  • Agreement takes effect January 1, 2026
  • Review after 3 years
  • Covers funding, salaries, autonomy, etc.
  • ASUU calls it "significant progress"

Students rejoice hopefully stable academic calendar ahead.

Other unions (SSANU, NASU) negotiations ongoing.
