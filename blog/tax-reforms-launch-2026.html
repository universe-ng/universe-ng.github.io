New Tax Reforms Officially Launch Today – January 1, 2026

By BTK • January 1, 2026

Happy New Year! 🎉 As we enter 2026, the biggest change happening today is the official start of Nigeria's major tax reforms.

What Launched Today

  • Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) begins operations — replacing FIRS in a historic restructure
  • Nigeria Tax Act & Tax Administration Act take full effect
  • Other laws from the package already active since late 2025

Government's Promise: Relief, Not Burden

Presidential Committee Chair Taiwo Oyedele: "These reforms are designed to provide relief to Nigerians, not to generate immediate revenue."

Key relief points:

  • ~98% of formal workers exempt from PAYE changes
  • ~97% of small businesses get lower or zero liability
  • Focus on high-income earners and large corporations
  • Simplified compliance for everyone

Why It Matters for Students

If you hustle (freelance, small sales, part-time work):

  • Most student-level income likely exempt
  • Easier tax filing if needed
  • Family businesses may pay less

Long-term goal: more revenue for education, roads, security things we all want.

Mixed Reactions

Some celebrate the relief. Others worry about implementation or hidden costs. The real test starts now.

2026 begins with change. Let's watch how it plays out.
