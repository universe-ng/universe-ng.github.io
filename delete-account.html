How to delete your UniVerse account

You can delete your account directly inside the UniVerse app by navigating to:
Settings > Account > Delete Account.

If you are unable to access the app, you can request manual deletion of your data by contacting our support team.

Request Manual Deletion

Please send an email with the subject "DELETE ACCOUNT".

  • Include your registered Email Address.
  • Include your Username.
  • We may ask for proof of ownership before deletion.

Click here to email support: devbtk00@gmail.com

Note: Data deletion is permanent. This will remove your profile, photos, comments, and UniCoins wallet balance. This action cannot be undone.
