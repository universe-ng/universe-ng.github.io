How to delete your UniVerse account

You can delete your account directly inside the UniVerse app by navigating to:
Settings > Account > Delete Account.

If you are unable to access the app, you can request manual deletion of your data by filling out the form below.

Request Manual Deletion

Note: Data deletion is permanent. This will remove your profile, photos, comments, and UniCoins wallet balance. This action cannot be undone.
